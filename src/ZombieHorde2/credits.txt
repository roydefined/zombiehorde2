CREDITS
================================================================================
Below is an list of people and third party projects who have been involved in the creation of this mod in one way or another.
It is possible that some of this information has been lost or forgotten about during development.
Want to make additions to this list? Then please consider contacting the information that can be found in `wadinfo.txt`.


MonsterOvich / Korshun
- Provided ACSUtils, a library with many ACS functions ranging from simple to
  advanced functions which were used as support on the source code.

Korshun
- For helping with bug support, general advice and tackling some serious issues
  that only somebody with great understanding of the internal engine could
  help with.
  
Loismustdie555
- For help with creating and converting the `STARTUP.dat` and `NOTCH.dat`
  graphics.

Captain J
- Creator of the `STARTUP.dat` graphic, weapon HUD icons and crouching
  zombie sprites. Additionally the creator of the new `console` icon above
  player heads and the shotgun casing sprites.

Metal_Spaghetti
- Creator of the updated zombie sprites.

Zedek the Plague Doctor
- Creator of all the updated weapon sprites. Also creator of much of the art
  that comes with the mod.

Tesefy / SoniK.O.Fan
- Creator of the initial zombie brightmaps.

Wocht11K
- Creator of the weapon brightmaps.

Aenima
- Creator of some of the things used in the decals, as released in `AeniPuffs`.

R3V3N93
- Support with 3d models.

Thfpjct
- Creator of THF-COMTEX, or ComTex textures.

40oz
- Creation of the 16-frame liquid animations.

DoomGappy
- The snowy edge texture.

kwc
- Some of the posters.

DooMAD
- Sewer entry textures.

DoomGappy
- A few dispenser textures.

Dox778
- Permission on using various props from Who Dun It: Reloaded.

Jimmy
- Creator of the new fonts

Zero X. Diamond
- Creator of the human sound effects. The sounds come from the original
  Brutal Doom voice sessions that were provided. Additionally they provided
  additional sounds to include, which is used for taunts, zombie sounds, and
  other things.

Tabijaky
- For choking on a water bottle (provided sound effects to add for the drowning
  pain and death sounds)

Agent_Ash
- Acid barrel sprites
- Modern Doom keys (the skull and key were split into two separate actors)

A_D_M_E_R_A_L
- Improved short/tall fire stick sprites.
- Improved door and elevator sounds.
