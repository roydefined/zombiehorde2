Other mod changes
------------------------------------------------

- Fixed an edge case where a player leaving right as the map starts could throw several errors.
- Fixed the damage event not properly checking for a valid player before setting new health.
- Improved the deathcam behavior in an attempt to fix issues that it has been having.
  Geometry or positioning often caused issues with the camera, and the player often ended up teleporting to a different position when exiting the camera.
  The camera now behaves with more checks that try to prevent this, and the camera ensures the player remains at their death position when exiting.
- Fixed the reward jingle played when receiving a powerup or item being audible to nearby players instead of just the player who received the reward.
- The radio is now turned off when the round ends and can no longer be turned on.
