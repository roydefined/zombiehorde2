MAP changes
------------------------------------------------

- [ZE02]: Fixed a broken teleport which didn't work for humans.
- [ZE04]:
    - Added a rock near the spawns to players can reach one of the human spawns.
    - Remove old 3d floor outlining on various 3d floors which are no longer required.
    - Fixed an issue with the cliff teleport not properly thrusting players at the end of the map.
    - Additionally the cliff teleport sequence will now freeze players when the cutscene plays.
- [ZE02B]: Fixed bad vertex slopes and a visual STARTAN texture on the map.
- [ZE05M]:
    - Made the train wheels solid.
    - Fixed an issue where you could get stuck in the big room before the reactor.
- [ZE08M]: Raised a rock so zombies can't cheese a resist by jumping on it from the water.
- [ZE09]: Fixed a missing teleport near the end of the map.
- [ZE32] / [ZM18] / [ZM22]: Fixed wrong textures that were caused by a previous texture improvement in the mod.
- [ZE32]: Various improvements were made to this map as it had quite a lot of flaws in terms of balance and general flow.
    - Fixed the map's music not looping correctly.
    - The gate before the keyed door now has a hole to defend against. This seemed to be the idea initially since the sectors already existed.
    - The key in the water instantly spawns instead of waiting until players reach the door.
    - The key now spawns in the second puddle closer to the door, with an indication of its location.
    - Said puddle is now also less deep so it's much easier to grab the key.
    - Fixed an issue where the map used barrel markers rather than actual barrels.
    - Various teleports on the map have been moved back as they always spawned players very close to where they fell or didn't teleport the player very reliably.
    - Changed the balcony jump near the end of the map to be more obvious and easier to navigate.
    - Added a bunch of very necessary impassable lines to prevent players from going out of bounds on various locations.
    - Made the trigger before you enter the sewer wider so you can't just walk around it.
    - The intro message near the first keyed door has been removed. Most players will have a key by the time they reach the door.
    - Removed a black wall after you jump the balconies which made it seem like you had to go there.
	- Replaced various invisible actors making an effect with proper actors that do the same thing. Notably flares and their lights and explosive barrels for the explosions.
- [ZM16]: Fix missing music when crazy weather starts.
- [ZM18]: The generator rooms can now be opened by everybody when opened once using their respective keycard.
- [ZM22]: Fix issue where the Jack's gun room could be opened without a key.
- [ZM27]:
    - The Jack's gun room can now be opened by everybody when opened once using the key.
    - Added some blocks to the water you fall into in the caves to make it easier to climb out of.

Other mod changes
------------------------------------------------

- Fixed the crush obituary icon being too big.
- Fixed missing states on ZM26's coin, ZM23's jewel and ZE12's cash. These caused the items no not work properly.
- Change damage handling to not trigger `EVENTPLAYERKILLEDBYPLAYER` if the killing player kills themselves (this can happen if a map damages a player when falling into a pit, for example).
- The Riotgun's knockback has been increased significantly (40 -> 140). Its spread has also been increased (5.0 -> 8.0).
- Fixed an issue where the game sometimes gave credit to a player when an actor with no source would kill somebody.
- Fixed an error shown in Ultimate DoomBuilder for a missing sprite on the Blot monster.
- Fixed a regression that caused players to retain their previous speed when getting infected or cured.
