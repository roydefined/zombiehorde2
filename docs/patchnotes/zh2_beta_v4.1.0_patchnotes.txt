MAP changes
------------------------------------------------

- [ZE27]:  Fix misaligned vertex slope actors causing slopes to not work.
           Also removed a very large glass 3d floor that existed in the sky and could be seen only by spectators that flew high enough.
- [ZE02M]: Fixed the first resist being passable.
           The map's polyobjects now have a system that prevents them from being blocked. Players that attempt to block it will get crushed and pushed away.
           To accompany this system a failsafe has also been added at the safezone to ensure players don't end up on the wrong side of the doors after the game ended.
           Lastly, the spotlights in front of the elevator have also been improved.
- [ZE12M]: Improved crushing behavior of the final room door.
- [ZE13M]: Improved crushing behavior of the dinner room and also the final room door.
- [ZM31M]: Removed a leftover debug message.

Other mod changes
